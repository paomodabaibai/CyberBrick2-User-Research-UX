Three-quarter front view, both feet and the open hand completely visible, entire robot within frame, generous clean margin. Landscape 4:3 composition. Cream white and friendly medium blue palette, charcoal eye accents. No text, no logo, no arrows, no dimensions, no extra views, no collage, no humans, no sci-fi armor, no wheels, no long legs. Prioritize readable simple short-leg biped shape and clear open-palm slap attachment.
