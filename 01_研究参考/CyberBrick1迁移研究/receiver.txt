## Introduction

You can combine this product with a **Multi-Function Core Board** to form a Cyberbrick receiver module. By pairing the **Remote Control Transmitter Shield** with another **Multi-Function Core Board**, you can create a Cyberbrick transmitter module. Connect joysticks and buttons to the transmitter module, and connect motors or servos to the receiver module, and you will have the minimal Cyberbrick remote control car project unit.
