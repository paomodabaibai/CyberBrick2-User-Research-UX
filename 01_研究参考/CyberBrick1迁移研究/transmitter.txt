## Introduction

You can combine this product with a **Multi-Function Core Board** to form a Cyberbrick transmitter module. By pairing a **Remote Control Receiver Shield** with another **Multi-Function Core Board**, you can create a Cyberbrick receiver module. Connect joysticks and buttons to the transmitter module, and motors or servos to the receiver module, and you will have a minimal Cyberbrick remote control car project unit.
